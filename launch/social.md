## LinkedIn

For a drug development assistant, correctly applying a specified calculation is useful. It also needs to be evaluated separately from predicting real pharmacology.

RealDrug compares fixed language-model endpoints with and without method cards on 15 generated exposure-calculation items and 20 generated Emax-rule dose-selection items. Under matched serving, mean exposure agreement within twofold changes from 0.867 to 1.000 across four models. Exact dose agreement changes from 0.940 to 0.980 across five models; its paired 95% difference interval, −0.020 to 0.100, includes no improvement.

Both reference sets are generated by procedures equivalent to the cards. High agreement therefore demonstrates adherence to those procedures, not clinical accuracy. The historical exposure and BOIN prompt wording also differs from the operational reference definitions, which limits attribution of the card effect.

The wider resource catalog includes ADMET, ligand ranking, free-energy scoring and cross-stage decisions. Those resources retain their own evidence status and are not presented as completed paired workflow evaluations.

The public artifacts include saved predictions, fixed scoring rules, reference generators and offline checks. We welcome scrutiny of both the results and the evaluation contract.

**RealDrug：把“按规则算对”与“预测真实药理”分开评测**

药物研发助手需要可靠地完成计算，但计算规则执行正确，并不等于对真实药理结果作出了正确预测。RealDrug明确区分这两件事。

当前完成的配对实验包括15个生成的暴露量计算题与20个生成的Emax规则剂量选择题。在匹配的服务设置下，4个模型的暴露量计算两倍范围内符合率从0.867升至1.000；5个模型的剂量精确符合率从0.940升至0.980，但差值95%区间为−0.020至0.100，包含零，不能据此宣称稳定改善。

参考答案由与方法卡等价的程序生成，因此这些结果衡量规则执行一致性，并不验证临床准确性。历史提示中的暴露峰值、BOIN及MTD术语，也与实际参考规则存在差异；方法卡同时提供了操作性定义，这会影响裸模型与提示模型比较的解释。

更广的任务资源仍然保留：ADMET、配体排序、结合自由能与跨阶段决策分别标明证据状态，不能把资源数量当成完整科研流程已经成功执行的证明。项目提供逐项预测、计分程序、参考生成器和离线复现检查。欢迎同行检查评测设计与结果。
